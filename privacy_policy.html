Privacy Policy

QuickDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuickDrop collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the QuickDrop platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to facilitate deliveries, including:

  • Name and phone number.
  • Optional email address.
  • Pickup and delivery addresses.
  • Receiver information.
  • Package details and instructions.
  • Payment processing information (status only).
  • Support communications.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used for the following purposes:

  • Manage orders and coordinate delivery logistics.
  • Verify One-Time Passwords (OTPs) for security.
  • Provide real-time tracking updates.
  • Process payments and verify proof of delivery.
  • Provide customer support and resolve issues.
  • Ensure safety and prevent fraud.

3. Delivery Information

We share relevant delivery information strictly with parties directly involved in the transaction, such as assigned delivery partners and designated receivers, to ensure successful fulfillment.

4. Location Information

We collect and utilize location tracking data solely for active delivery operations to provide accurate ETAs and route optimization.

5. Payment Information

We only process transaction status data. QuickDrop does not store or process sensitive credit card details on our servers; this is handled securely by third-party payment gateways.

6. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with Senders, Receivers, Delivery Partners, trusted service providers, and legal authorities when necessary to comply with the law or protect rights.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill our legal, accounting, or reporting obligations.

9. Your Choices

You have the right to update your account information directly within the app. For data deletion requests, please contact QuickDrop support.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the minimum legal age requirements of their respective jurisdictions. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be made available through the platform, and continued use constitutes acceptance of the new terms.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us through our designated support channels within the app.
